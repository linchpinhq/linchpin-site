# Listicle outreach — get Linchpin into existing "best X" pages

From the baseline AI-search tests, these 4 listicles ranked highly for "best Devin alternative / self-hosted AI agent" queries but did NOT include Linchpin. Each is a backlink and a citation source for AI engines.

## Template: email/Twitter outreach

Most of these sites accept submissions via contact form, Twitter DM, or LinkedIn. The pitch is identical; the channel varies.

**Subject:** Suggestion for your "Best Open Source Devin Alternatives" article

**Body:**
```
Hi,

I came across your article "[exact article title]" while researching
open-source agent runtimes. Solid roundup.

One project that fits the category but isn't in the list:
**Linchpin** — https://linchpin.work — an open-source, self-hostable
runtime for managed AI agents (Apache-2.0). Different shape from
OpenHands or Cline; closer to "the runtime layer Devin runs on" than to
Devin itself. Single VM, docker compose, per-session Docker sandbox,
OpenRouter and Ollama for models, MCP support.

If you're updating the article, this might be worth a mention:
https://linchpin.work/compare/devin (side-by-side with Devin specifically)

GitHub: https://github.com/linchpinhq/linchpin

Thanks for the work on the roundup — it's how I find half the projects
I end up using.

— [your name]
```

## Notes

- Personalize the first line. "Solid roundup" with the article title is bare minimum. If you can reference a specific entry, do.
- Don't pitch all 4 in one week. Stagger ~5 days apart.
- Some authors will reply asking for a guest writeup or a sponsored mention. Sponsored gets you in the article; whether that's worth the price depends on the site's traffic (check Ahrefs / Similarweb numbers first).
- Authors who write these articles often update them quarterly. If they say "next update in [month]," diary it.

## Backup angle: "I wrote a comparison"

If the cold outreach lands flat, post the relevant linchpin.work/compare/* page on Reddit (r/LocalLLaMA, r/selfhosted, r/programming) as "I made a comparison of Linchpin and Devin [or whatever]." The Reddit thread becomes its own citation source AND tends to prompt listicle authors to re-find your project organically.
